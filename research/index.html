Research

PhD researcher at the University of Eastern Finland (since 03/2025), working at the intersection of eye tracking, web technologies and AI.

Profile

  • PhD / doctoral researcher, University of Eastern Finland (UEF), Joensuu — since 03/2025.
  • Research Associate, project REACH, OTH Regensburg — eye-tracking glasses and web usability.
  • Chair & main organiser of the METR workshop (Metrics in Eye Tracking Research) at ETRA 2026 — with a community of ~68 researchers working on metric standardisation.
  • Research stays in Finland, Morocco, the Netherlands and Spain.
